## Install

1. Open the `.dmg` and drag Sharks to Applications (replace the previous version if asked).
2. The first time you open it, macOS asks whether you want to open an app downloaded from the internet: click **Open**. Sharks is signed with an Apple Developer ID certificate and notarized by Apple.

Your projects, hours, passwords and settings stay as they were.

## What's new

- **Abyss theme:** a deep-sea theme that unlocks after 100 days using Sharks (they don't need to be in a row). Your days so far already count. See your progress in Settings → General.
- **Passwords:** click the field to copy a password without showing it; the eye shows it for 15 seconds. Edit and delete are always visible, and the project filter is easier to use.
- **Commands:** create and edit them in a dialog. Empty searches tell you why and let you clear the filters.
- **Project look:** more than 200 icons and 250 emojis, by category.
- **Tooltips** no longer get cut off by cards, lists or the sidebar.
- **Language:** menus, the menu bar icon, error messages, notices and dates now follow the language chosen in Settings, in English and Spanish.
- **Same words everywhere:** Start and Stop for projects, services and Home.
- **Shortcuts:** ⌘7 opens Ports, and Settings lists it.
- **Updates:** Sharks only tells you about a version that has an installer for your computer.
