## Install

1. Open the `.dmg` and drag Sharks to Applications.
2. The first time you open it, macOS asks whether you want to open an app downloaded from the internet: click **Open**. Sharks is signed with an Apple Developer ID certificate and notarized by Apple.

## What's in it

- **Projects:** start every service of a project in one click, each in its own terminal, with its localhost links at hand.
- **Command detection:** reads `package.json`, Docker Compose, Makefile and the README, and asks you to approve each command before it runs.
- **Time:** counted only while a project is running or you focus on it, and paused when you are idle.
- **Focus:** work blocks with breaks and the countdown in the menu bar.
- **Agenda and tasks**, with Google Calendar.
- **Command dictionary** for the ones you always forget.
- **Passwords and tokens** encrypted with your master password (scrypt + AES-256-GCM), with Touch ID.
- **Read-only integrations:** GitHub (pull requests and alerts), Google Drive and Google Calendar.
- **Docker Compose** and **ports in use**.
- **Your data stays on your Mac**, with daily and weekly backups.
- In English and Spanish.
